# Governance

## Commitments

The project commits to evidence transparency, traceable provenance, global
source inclusion, worker and public-interest visibility, resistance to
institutional or commercial capture, publication of unresolved disagreement,
correction over reputation protection, and accessible public reuse. These are
operational commitments, not a claim of political neutrality.

## Roles and decisions

Maintainers protect the evidence contract, review releases, disclose conflicts,
and document decisions. Routine changes use pull-request review. Schema-breaking
changes require a public proposal, migration notes, and a major release.
Maintainers with a material conflict recuse themselves.

Disputes are recorded with their evidence and reviewed against the published
method, not contributor status. Appeals receive a written rationale. Moderation
protects participation without suppressing relevant contrary evidence.

## Corrections, removal, and privacy

Confirmed corrections receive priority and propagate through derivatives.
Records are superseded rather than silently rewritten across releases. Removal
requests consider law, safety, privacy, provenance integrity, and public value.
Personal data is excluded unless essential, lawful, proportionate, and already
part of an authoritative public record.

## Automation and succession

Automated contributors are identifiable, reproducible, and subject to the same
evidence bar. Release authority belongs to active maintainers through protected
Git tags and GitHub environments. If maintainers become inactive for 90 days,
documented active contributors may nominate successors through a public issue
and two-week comment period.
