# Roadmap

## v0.1 — vertical slice

Four standards, canonical schemas, crosswalk, machine indexes, SQLite/CSV,
Pages, releases, validation, contribution workflows, and one downstream example.

## Later, after demonstrated demand

- non-English summaries reviewed by fluent contributors;
- implementation-level tool compatibility evidence;
- Parquet distribution and package registry publication;
- OCI and archival mirrors;
- additional provenance standards and ecosystem profiles;
- conversion loss studies and verification reproductions.

No topic expands until its source quality, update cadence, dispute handling, and
correction propagation are maintainable.
