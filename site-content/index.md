# Open Provenance Knowledge

Evidence-backed, machine-readable knowledge about open software provenance
standards.

The v0.1.0 vertical slice maps SLSA 1.2, in-toto Attestation Framework 1.2,
SPDX 3.0.1, and CycloneDX 1.7. It distinguishes evidence from interpretation
and preserves disagreement, provenance, freshness, and independent trust
dimensions.

This is not a ranking, certification, endorsement, or claim of consensus.
